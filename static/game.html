    Cocktail Menu & Scoring
    First match wins — reach 40 pts to trigger the last round.
    How to Play
    Winning
    • Reach the target to start the last round: 40 pts with two players, 35 with three, 30 with four. The round finishes so everyone has had the same number of turns; most points wins (a tie goes to whoever is earliest in turn order).
    • Claim 3 karaoke cards and you win on the spot.
    • Last one standing wins when everyone else is in hospital, wet or gone home.
    Your Turn

    One main action: take ingredients, sell your cups (one or both), drink a cup, or go for a wee.

    Free actions, each once a turn, before or after: claim a card, clear the orders (drunk ≥3), swipe the ability cards (drunk ≥2), and whatever your free-action cards give you. Pouring or drinking from a Store card is free too.

    Your turn ends when your main action is done and nothing free is left that you could use. You can end it early after your main action.

    Take Ingredients

    Take exactly 3 + your drunk level, from the open display, the specials tray, blind from the bag, or any mix. Put each one in a cup (5 spirits and mixers, plus up to 2 specials) or drink it before taking more. Nothing goes back.

    Drunk level (changes once, after the whole take)
    • Each spirit drunk, or bitters, cointreau or vermouth: +1
    • Drank none of those → −1 per mixer, lemon or sugar (minimum 0)
    • Drunk level > 5 → hospital, out of the game
    • Bladder overflows → you wet yourself, out of the game
    Specials

    Two each of bitters, cointreau, lemon, sugar and vermouth are in the bag. One that comes out goes to the specials tray and the drawing carries on, so the display always shows 5 spirits and mixers and a blind draw never gives you a special.

    Anyone can take a special from the tray as part of their take: into a cup (up to 2 per cup) or to drink. A cup with a special in it only sells as the cocktail it makes. Specials never count toward a card, except that one special pays for a specialist card (see Claim a Card).

    Sell Your Cups

    Sell one cup or both in one action. Everything in them goes back in the bag.

    • One spirit + one kind of valid mixer: 1 pt
    • Two of the same spirit + one kind of valid mixer: 3 pts
    • Tequila Slammer (exactly 2×Tequila): 3 pts
    • Cocktail: 10 pts  |  Long Island Iced Tea: 15 pts

    Cocktails must match the recipe exactly, specials included. If an order on the table wants your drink you also get its bonus (+2 simple, +3 slammer, +4 cocktail, +5 Long Island; never doubled).

    Go for a Wee

    Only with something in your bladder. Empty it into the bag and sober up 1 level. Each wee seals a bladder space with a toilet token, down to 4 spaces.

    Claim a Card (free)

    Claim a karaoke or ability card whose cost is in your bladder. The cost is paid: those ingredients go from your bladder back into the bag. Stored spirits don’t count.

    A specialist card can be paid with 2 of its spirit, or 1 of its special: bitters for whisky, cointreau for tequila, vermouth for vodka, sugar for rum, lemon for gin. A Store card pays 1 spirit and the rest of that spirit in your bladder moves onto it.

    Karaoke cards aren’t replaced; a claimed ability card is replaced from the deck.

    Clearing Rows (free)

    Clear the orders at drunk ≥3, or swipe the ability cards at drunk ≥2, once a turn each. All three cards go to the bottom of their deck and three new ones are dealt. The karaoke row is never cleared.

    Drink a Cup

    Drink the entire contents of one of your cups. The same drunk and bladder rules apply as when taking ingredients.
